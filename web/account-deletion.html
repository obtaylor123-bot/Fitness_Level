Request Fitness Level account deletion

This page lets you request deletion without reinstalling the app. We will use your request to locate and verify the account before deleting its cloud profile, workouts, XP, optional weight records, and consent record.

Send the request from the email address used for your Fitness Level account. Do not include your password, sign-in code, or Google credentials.

Email an account deletion request

If you cannot access that email address, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and ask for the alternate identity-verification process. A request is not complete until the account owner confirms it.
